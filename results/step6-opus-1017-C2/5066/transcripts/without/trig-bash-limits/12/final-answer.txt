What the two diagnostics showed:
1. **Large output:** the command ran, but the tool cut the output off with "(Output was truncated due to length limit)". The message didn't say what the limit was, so I can't tell you how many of the ~40,000 characters came back.
2. **Timeout:** the command was stopped at 2000 ms with "(Command timed out after 2000 ms)" while `sleep 5` was still running. The tests never ran, and the tool showed no partial output.
