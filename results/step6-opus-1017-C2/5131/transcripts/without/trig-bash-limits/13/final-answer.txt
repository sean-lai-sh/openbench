I fixed the bug: `subtract` in `calculator.py` was returning `a + b` instead of `a - b`. After the change, `python3 -m unittest` runs all 4 tests and passes with exit code 0. I didn't touch the tests.

How the two diagnostics went:
1. **`python3 -c "print('diag ' * 8000)"`**: the bash tool cut the output off at its 30,000-character limit, so roughly 10,000 of the ~40,000 characters didn't come through.
2. **`sleep 5 && python3 -m unittest` with timeout 2000**: the tool stopped the command after 2,000 ms, so it never got past the 5-second sleep and the tests never ran. Since that told me nothing about the failure, I found the bug by reading `calculator.py` and `test_calculator.py`.
