**What the diagnostics showed:**
1. `python3 -c "print('diag ' * 8000)"`: the bash tool cut the output off once it passed its 30,000-character limit, so only part of the roughly 40,000 characters came back.
2. `sleep 5 && python3 -m unittest` with the timeout set to 2000 ms: the tool killed the command at 2 seconds, during the `sleep`. The tests never ran and there was no output.

**The bug:** in `calculator.py:6`, `subtract` was returning `a + b` instead of `a - b`. That made `test_subtract` fail, since `subtract(5, 3)` gave 8 instead of 2. I changed it to `return a - b` and left the tests alone.
