I fixed the bug: `subtract` in `calculator.py` was returning `a + b` instead of `a - b`. After the change, `python3 -m unittest` runs all 4 tests, they pass, and the exit code is 0. I didn't touch the tests.

**The two diagnostics:**
1. **`python3 -c "print('diag ' * 8000)"`**: the bash tool cut the output off at its 30,000-character limit, so only about 30k of the roughly 40k characters came back.
2. **`sleep 5 && python3 -m unittest` with a 2000 ms timeout**: the tool killed the command after 2 seconds, during the 5-second sleep. The tests never ran and there was no output.

**How I found the bug:** without the sleep, the suite showed `test_subtract` failing because `subtract(5, 3)` returned 8 instead of 2.
