The crash is fixed. `python3 main.py` now exits cleanly and prints `Total: 5.0`.

The cause was a typo in `settings.json`: the key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `pricing.get_rate()` looks up `"rate"` with `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, which returns `None` for a missing key.
