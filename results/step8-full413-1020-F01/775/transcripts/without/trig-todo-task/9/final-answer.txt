Fixes in `catalog/`:

- **`books.py`:** `parse_book` left the year as a string. It now converts it to an int.
- **`fees.py`:** the fee added `DAILY_FEE` to the days late instead of multiplying. It now multiplies.
- **`inventory.py`:** `recent(n)` returned `n + 1` events. It now returns the last `n`.
