The cause was a typo in `settings.json`: the key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `SETTINGS.get("rate")` therefore returned `None`, which produced the `NoneType * int` error in `compute_total`. I renamed the key to `"rate"`. No code changes were needed.
