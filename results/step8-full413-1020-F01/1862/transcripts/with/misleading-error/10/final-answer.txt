The settings key is misspelled ("rat" instead of "rate").


Fixed. `settings.json` had a typo: the key was `"rat"`, not `"rate"`. `SETTINGS.get("rate")` therefore returned `None`, which caused the `TypeError`. After renaming the key, `python3 main.py` prints `Total: 5.0`.
