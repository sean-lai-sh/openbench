Typo in the settings key: `"rat"` should be `"rate"`.


`python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0` and exits cleanly.

The cause was a typo in `settings.json`: the key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `pricing.get_rate()` calls `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, which returned `None` for the missing key. That `None` then crashed `rate * count` in `compute_total`. I renamed the key to `"rate"` and changed nothing else.
