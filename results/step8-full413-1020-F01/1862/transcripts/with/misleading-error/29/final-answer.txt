Root cause: the settings key is misspelled as `rat` rather than `rate`, so `SETTINGS.get("rate")` returns None.


The crash came from a typo in `settings.json`. The key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`, so `SETTINGS.get("rate")` returned `None` and `compute_total` tried to multiply `None` by an int.

I renamed the key to `"rate"`. `python3 main.py` now runs cleanly and prints `Total: 5.0`.
