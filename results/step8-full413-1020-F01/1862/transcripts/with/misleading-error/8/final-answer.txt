Typo in settings key: "rat" should be "rate".


`python3 main.py` now exits cleanly and prints `Total: 5.0`.

The cause was a typo in `settings.json`: the key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `get_rate()` in `app/pricing.py` calls `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, which returned `None` because the key didn't exist. That `None` then failed in `rate * count`. I renamed the key to `"rate"` and didn't change any code.
