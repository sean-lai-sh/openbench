Typo in settings key: `rat` instead of `rate`.


Fixed. `settings.json` had a typo in its key (`"rat"` instead of `"rate"`). `SETTINGS.get("rate")` therefore returned `None`, which caused the `TypeError`. `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0`.
