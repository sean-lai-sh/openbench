The cause was a typo in `settings.json`: the key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `pricing.get_rate()` looks up `"rate"`, so it returned `None`, and `compute_total` then tried to multiply `None` by `10`. I renamed the key to `"rate"` and left the code alone.
