Typo in settings key: `rat` instead of `rate`.


`python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0` and exits successfully.

The cause was a typo in `settings.json`: the key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `pricing.get_rate()` looks up `"rate"`, so it got `None`. That `None` is what `compute_total` tried to multiply by the count. I renamed the key to `"rate"` and left the code alone.
