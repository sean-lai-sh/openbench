Typo in the settings key: `rat` instead of `rate`.


The crash came from a typo in `settings.json`: the key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `SETTINGS.get("rate")` therefore returned `None`, and `None * 10` raised the `TypeError`. I renamed the key to `"rate"`, and `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0`.

I didn't change any Python code. `pricing.get_rate()` still returns `None` silently when the key is missing, so a similar typo would give the same confusing error again.
