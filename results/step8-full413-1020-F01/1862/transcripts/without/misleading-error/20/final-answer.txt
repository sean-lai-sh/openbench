Typo in the settings key: `rat` instead of `rate`.


`python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0` and exits cleanly.

The cause was a typo in `settings.json`: the key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `pricing.get_rate()` looks up `"rate"`, so it got `None`, and `None * count` raised the `TypeError`. I renamed the key and changed nothing else.
