Typo in settings key: "rat" should be "rate".


The crash came from a typo in `settings.json`. The key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`, so `SETTINGS.get("rate")` returned `None` and `None * count` raised the `TypeError`. I renamed the key to `"rate"`, and `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0`.
