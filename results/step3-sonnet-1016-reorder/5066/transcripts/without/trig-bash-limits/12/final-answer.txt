Both diagnostics ran as written:
1. The `'diag ' * 8000` command printed about 40,000 characters, and the bash tool truncated the output at its length limit.
2. `sleep 5 && python3 -m unittest` with `timeout` set to 2000 timed out after 2000 ms, because the sleep is longer than the timeout. The tests never ran in that call, so I ran them separately to see the failure.
