I fixed the crash, and `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0`.

- **Cause:** `settings.json` had the key misspelled as `"rat"`. `get_rate()` in `app/pricing.py:8` calls `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, which returned `None`. That `None` then hit `rate * count` at `app/report.py:9`.
- **Fix:** I renamed `"rat"` to `"rate"` in `settings.json`.
- **Subagent id:** The first task call didn't return an id, and the tool has no parameter for passing one. The second call therefore went to a fresh subagent instead of continuing the first. Its answer matched the first one.
