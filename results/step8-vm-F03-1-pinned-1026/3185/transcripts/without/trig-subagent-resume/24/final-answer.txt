- **Cause:** `settings.json` had the key `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `get_rate()` in `app/pricing.py:8` calls `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, which returns `None` for a missing key. That `None` then hit `rate * count` in `app/report.py:9`.
- **Fix:** I renamed the key to `"rate"` in `settings.json`.
