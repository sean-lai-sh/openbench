The cause was a typo in `settings.json`: the key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `get_rate()` in `app/pricing.py:8` calls `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, which returned `None` when the key was missing. That `None` reached `rate * count` in `app/report.py:9` and raised the `TypeError`. I renamed the key to `"rate"`.
