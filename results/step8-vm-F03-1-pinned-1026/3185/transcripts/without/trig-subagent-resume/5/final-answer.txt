- **Cause:** `settings.json:2` had the key `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `app/pricing.py:8` calls `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, which returned `None` instead of raising an error. That `None` reached `rate * count` at `app/report.py:9`.
- **Fix:** I renamed the key to `"rate"`. I left `.get("rate")` in `app/pricing.py:8` as it was, but it still hides this kind of mistake if you want to tighten it later.
