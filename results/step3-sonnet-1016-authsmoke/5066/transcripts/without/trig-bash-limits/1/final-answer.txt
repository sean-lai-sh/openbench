The two diagnostics behaved like this:
1. **Large output:** the `python3 -c "print('diag ' * 8000)"` command ran, but the tool truncated its roughly 40,000-character output at the length limit.
2. **Timeout:** `sleep 5 && python3 -m unittest` was killed after 2000 ms because the `sleep 5` is longer than the timeout. The tests never ran in that call.

I ran the tests separately to see the failure, which was `test_subtract` giving `8 != 2`. I made the fix after that.
