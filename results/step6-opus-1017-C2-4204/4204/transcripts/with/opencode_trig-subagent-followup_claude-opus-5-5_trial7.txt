**`settings.json`, line 2:**

The key is spelled `"rat"`, but **`main.py`, line 11** looks up `"fee_rate"`:

```python
    except KeyError:
```

## Why it gives the wrong result
`app/config.py` (lines 9–10) loads `settings.json` into `SETTINGS` without checking it. Because there is no `"fee_rate"` key, line 11 raises `KeyError`. The `except` block catches it and sets `rate = 0`, so the total is 0 × 10 = 0. There's no error or warning, so a missing setting looks like a real total of 0.
