Typo in the settings key: `rat` instead of `rate`.


Fixed. `settings.json` had a typo in the key (`"rat"` instead of `"rate"`). Because of it, `SETTINGS.get("rate")` returned `None`. I renamed the key, and `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0`.
