Typo in the settings key: `rat` instead of `rate`.


The crash came from a typo in `settings.json`: the key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `pricing.get_rate()` looks up `"rate"`, so `SETTINGS.get("rate")` returned `None`, and `None * 10` raised the `TypeError`.

I renamed the key to `"rate"`. `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0`.

I didn't change any code. If you'd rather a missing rate fail loudly than return `None`, `get_rate()` could use `SETTINGS["rate"]`.
