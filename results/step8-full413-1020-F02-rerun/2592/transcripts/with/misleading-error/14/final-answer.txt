The key is misspelled: `rat` instead of `rate`.


`settings.json` had a typo: the key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `pricing.get_rate()` looks up `"rate"`, so it got `None`, and `None * 10` raised the `TypeError`.

I renamed the key to `"rate"`. `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0`.
