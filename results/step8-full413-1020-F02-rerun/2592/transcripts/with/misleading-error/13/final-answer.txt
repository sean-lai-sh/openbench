Typo in settings key: `rat` → `rate`.


The crash was caused by a typo in `settings.json`: the key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `pricing.get_rate()` therefore returned `None`. I renamed the key to `"rate"`, and `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0`.
