Typo in settings key: "rat" should be "rate".


The crash came from a typo in `settings.json`. The key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `SETTINGS.get("rate")` therefore returned `None`, and `rate * count` failed. I renamed the key to `"rate"`.

`python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0` and exits cleanly.
