Key typo in settings.json: "rat" should be "rate".


`python3 main.py` now exits cleanly and prints `Total: 5.0`.

The cause was a typo in `settings.json`: the key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `pricing.get_rate()` looks up `"rate"` with `.get()`, which returned `None` for the missing key. That `None` then failed in `rate * count`, so the error showed up in `report.py` even though the bug was in the settings file.

I renamed the key to `"rate"` and didn't change any code.
