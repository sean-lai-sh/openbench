Key typo: `"rat"` should be `"rate"`.


`python3 main.py` now exits cleanly and prints `Total: 5.0`.

The cause was a typo in `settings.json`: the key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `pricing.get_rate()` looks up `"rate"` with `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, so it quietly returned `None`. That `None` then caused the `TypeError` when `compute_total` multiplied it by the count. I renamed the key to `"rate"` and changed nothing else.
