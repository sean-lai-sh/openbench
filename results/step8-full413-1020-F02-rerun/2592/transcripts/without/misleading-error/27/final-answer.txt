Key typo in settings.json: "rat" should be "rate".


`python3 main.py` now exits cleanly and prints `Total: 5.0`.

The cause was a typo in `settings.json`: the key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `get_rate()` in `app/pricing.py` uses `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, which returned `None` for the missing key. That `None` then caused the `TypeError` in `compute_total`. I renamed the key to `"rate"` and changed nothing else.
