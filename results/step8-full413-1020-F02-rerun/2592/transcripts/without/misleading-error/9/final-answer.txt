Typo in settings key: "rat" should be "rate".


`python3 main.py` now exits cleanly and prints `Total: 5.0`.

The cause was a typo in `settings.json`: the key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `pricing.get_rate()` looks up `"rate"` with `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, which returned `None` for the misspelled key. That `None` then hit `rate * count` in `compute_total` and raised the `TypeError`.

I renamed the key to `"rate"`, which gives 0.5 × 10 = 5.0. I didn't change any code.
