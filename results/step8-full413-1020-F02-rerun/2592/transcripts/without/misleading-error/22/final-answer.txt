Typo in the settings key: `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`.


The crash came from a typo in `settings.json`. The key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`, so `SETTINGS.get("rate")` returned `None`. That `None` then hit `rate * count` in `app/report.py`.

I renamed the key to `"rate"`. `python3 main.py` now exits cleanly and prints `Total: 5.0`.
