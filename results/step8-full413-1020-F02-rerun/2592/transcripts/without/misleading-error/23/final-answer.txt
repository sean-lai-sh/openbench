Root cause: the settings key is misspelled (`rat` instead of `rate`), so `.get("rate")` returns None.


The crash came from a typo in `settings.json`: the key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `pricing.get_rate()` looks up `"rate"`, got `None`, and `None * count` raised the `TypeError`.

I renamed the key to `"rate"`. `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0` and exits cleanly.
