Key typo in settings: `rat` should be `rate`.


`settings.json` had a typo: the key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `SETTINGS.get("rate")` therefore returned `None`, which caused the `TypeError`. I renamed the key to `"rate"`. `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0`.
