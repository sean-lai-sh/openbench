Typo in settings.json key: "rat" → "rate".


The crash came from a typo in `settings.json`: the key was `"rat"`, but `app/pricing.py` looks up `"rate"`. `SETTINGS.get("rate")` therefore returned `None`, which caused the `TypeError` in `compute_total`.

I renamed the key to `"rate"`. `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0` and exits successfully.
