Key typo: "rat" instead of "rate". Fixing it gives 0.5*10 = 5.0.


`python3 main.py` now exits successfully and prints `Total: 5.0`.

The cause was a typo in `settings.json`: the key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `pricing.get_rate()` calls `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, which returned `None` because the key didn't exist. That `None` is what `compute_total` then tried to multiply by the count. I renamed the key to `"rate"`, so the total is 0.5 × 10 = 5.0. No code changes were needed.
