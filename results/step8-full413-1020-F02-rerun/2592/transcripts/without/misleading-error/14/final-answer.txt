The settings key is misspelled as `rat`. It should be `rate`.


`python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0` and exits cleanly.

`settings.json` had the key misspelled as `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `pricing.get_rate()` looks up `"rate"` with `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, which returns `None` for a missing key. That `None` then hit `rate * count` in `compute_total` and caused the `TypeError`. I renamed the key to `"rate"` and changed nothing else.
