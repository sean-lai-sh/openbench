The crash is fixed, and `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0`.

The cause was a typo in `settings.json`: the key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `pricing.get_rate()` looks up `"rate"`, so it returned `None`, and `None * count` raised the `TypeError` in `compute_total`. I renamed the key to `"rate"`. I made no code changes.
