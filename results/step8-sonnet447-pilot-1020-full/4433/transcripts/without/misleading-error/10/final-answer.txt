The crash came from a typo in `settings.json`: the key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `pricing.get_rate()` calls `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, which quietly returned `None`. That `None` then failed in `rate * count`.

I renamed the key to `"rate"`. `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0` and exits cleanly.

I left the code alone. If you want a missing key to fail loudly instead of returning `None`, `get_rate()` could use `SETTINGS["rate"]`.
