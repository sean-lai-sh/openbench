The crash came from a typo in `settings.json`. The key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `SETTINGS.get("rate")` in `app/pricing.py` therefore returned `None`, and `compute_total` then tried `None * 10`.

I renamed the key to `"rate"`. `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0` and exits cleanly.

I left the code alone. `get_rate()` still returns `None` silently when the key is missing, so a similar typo would give the same confusing `TypeError`. Switching it to `SETTINGS["rate"]` would make that fail with a clear `KeyError`. I haven't made that change.
