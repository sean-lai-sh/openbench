`python3 main.py` now exits cleanly and prints `Total: 5.0`.

The cause was a typo in `settings.json`. The key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `pricing.get_rate()` calls `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, which returned `None` because the key didn't exist. That `None` then reached `rate * count` in `report.py` and raised the `TypeError`.

I renamed the key to `"rate"` in `settings.json` and changed no code.
