The script now runs and prints `Total: 5.0`.

The cause was a typo in `settings.json`. The key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`, so `SETTINGS.get("rate")` in `app/pricing.py` returned `None`. That `None` then reached `rate * count` in `compute_total` and raised the `TypeError`. I renamed the key to `"rate"` and changed no code.

One thing to be aware of: `get_rate()` still returns `None` quietly when the key is missing. If you want a typo like this to fail with a clear message, change it to `SETTINGS["rate"]`. I left that alone because you didn't ask for it.
