I fixed it. `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0` and exits cleanly.

The crash came from a typo in `settings.json`. The key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `pricing.get_rate()` looks up `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, which returned `None` because the key didn't exist. That `None` then failed in `rate * count` in `app/report.py`. I renamed the key to `"rate"`, and no code changed.
