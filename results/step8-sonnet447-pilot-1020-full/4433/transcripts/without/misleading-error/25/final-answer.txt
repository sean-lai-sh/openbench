The crash came from a typo in `settings.json`. The key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `pricing.get_rate()` calls `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, which returned `None` because the key wasn't there. That `None` then failed in `rate * count` in `report.py`.

I renamed the key to `"rate"`. `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0` and exits cleanly.

I didn't change any code. If you'd rather a missing rate fail with a clear error than quietly return `None`, `get_rate()` could raise when the key is absent.
