`python3 main.py` now exits cleanly and prints `Total: 5.0`.

`settings.json` had a typo: the key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `pricing.get_rate()` calls `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, which returned `None` because the key didn't exist. That `None` is what crashed `rate * count` in `compute_total`. I renamed the key to `"rate"` and changed no code.

`.get()` hides this kind of mistake by returning `None` for a missing key. If you'd like a missing `rate` to fail with a clear error, use `SETTINGS["rate"]` in `get_rate()`. I left it as it was.
