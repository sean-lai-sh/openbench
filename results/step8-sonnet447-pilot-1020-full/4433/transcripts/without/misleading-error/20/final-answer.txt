`python3 main.py` now exits cleanly and prints `Total: 5.0`.

`settings.json` had the key misspelled as `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `pricing.get_rate()` calls `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, which returned `None` when the key wasn't found. That `None` then crashed in `rate * count`. I renamed the key to `"rate"` in `settings.json` and left the code alone.
