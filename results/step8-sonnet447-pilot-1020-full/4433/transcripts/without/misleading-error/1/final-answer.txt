The crash came from a typo in `settings.json`. The key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `pricing.get_rate()` looks up `"rate"` with `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, so it returned `None`. That `None` then hit `rate * count` in `compute_total`.

I renamed the key to `"rate"`. `python3 main.py` now runs cleanly and prints `Total: 5.0`.

I didn't change any code. If you'd rather a missing rate fail with a clear message than a `TypeError`, `get_rate()` could raise an error when the key is absent. I left that alone since you didn't ask for it.
