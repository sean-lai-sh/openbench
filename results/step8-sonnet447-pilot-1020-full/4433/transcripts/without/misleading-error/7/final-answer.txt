`python3 main.py` now exits cleanly and prints `Total: 5.0`.

The cause was a typo in `settings.json`. The key was `"rat"`, but `app/pricing.py` looks up `"rate"`. `SETTINGS.get("rate")` therefore returned `None`, which produced the `NoneType * int` error in `compute_total`. I renamed the key to `"rate"`. I didn't change any code.

`get_rate()` still returns `None` silently when the key is missing. If you want a future typo like this to fail with a clear error, use `SETTINGS["rate"]` instead.
