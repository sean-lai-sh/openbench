`python3 main.py` now exits cleanly and prints `Total: 5.0`.

The cause was a typo in `settings.json`: the key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `pricing.get_rate()` calls `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, which quietly returned `None`. That `None` then reached `rate * count` in `compute_total` and raised the `TypeError`. I renamed the key to `"rate"` and changed nothing in the code.

`get_rate()` is why the error pointed at the wrong place. If you'd rather it fail loudly on a missing key, use `SETTINGS["rate"]` so you get a `KeyError` naming the key. I left that alone since you didn't ask for it.
