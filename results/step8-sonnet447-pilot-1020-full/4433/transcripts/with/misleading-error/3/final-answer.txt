`python3 main.py` now runs cleanly and prints `Total: 5.0`.

The cause was a typo in `settings.json`. The key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`, so `SETTINGS.get("rate")` in `app/pricing.py` returned `None`. That `None` is what crashed the multiplication in `compute_total`. I renamed the key to `"rate"` and changed nothing else.
