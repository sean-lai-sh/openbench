`python3 main.py` now exits cleanly and prints `Total: 5.0`.

**Cause:** `settings.json` had a typo in the key, `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `pricing.get_rate()` calls `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, which returned `None` because the key wasn't there. `compute_total` then tried `None * 10`, which raised the `TypeError`.

**Fix:** I renamed the key to `"rate"` in `settings.json`. I didn't change any code.
