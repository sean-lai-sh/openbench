`python3 main.py` now exits successfully and prints `Total: 5.0`.

The cause was a typo in `settings.json`. The key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`, so `SETTINGS.get("rate")` in `app/pricing.py` returned `None`. That `None` then hit `rate * count` in `compute_total` and raised the `TypeError`. I renamed the key to `"rate"` and left the code alone.

The `.get("rate")` call hid the problem by returning `None` for a missing key. Indexing with `SETTINGS["rate"]` would have raised a `KeyError` that named the missing key. I didn't make that change.
