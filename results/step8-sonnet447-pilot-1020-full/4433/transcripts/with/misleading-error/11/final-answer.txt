`python3 main.py` now exits cleanly and prints `Total: 5.0`.

The cause was a typo in `settings.json`. The key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `pricing.get_rate()` calls `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, which returned `None` for the misspelled key. That `None` then failed in `rate * count` in `compute_total`. I renamed the key to `"rate"` and changed nothing else.
