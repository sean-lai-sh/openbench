`python3 main.py` now exits cleanly and prints `Total: 5.0`.

The cause was a typo in `settings.json`: the key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `pricing.get_rate()` calls `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, which returned `None` because the key didn't exist. That `None` is what `compute_total` then tried to multiply by `count`. I renamed the key to `"rate"`, so the total is 0.5 × 10 = 5.0. I made no code changes.
