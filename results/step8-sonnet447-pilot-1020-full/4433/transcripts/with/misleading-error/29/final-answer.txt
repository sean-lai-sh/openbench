`python3 main.py` now exits cleanly and prints `Total: 5.0`.

The cause was a typo in `settings.json`: the key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `pricing.get_rate()` calls `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, which returned `None` because the key didn't exist. That `None` then crashed `rate * count` in `app/report.py`. I renamed the key to `"rate"`, and no code changed.

`get_rate()` returns `None` silently when the key is missing. If you want this kind of typo to fail loudly next time, changing it to `SETTINGS["rate"]` would raise a `KeyError` naming the missing key. I left it alone since you didn't ask for it.
