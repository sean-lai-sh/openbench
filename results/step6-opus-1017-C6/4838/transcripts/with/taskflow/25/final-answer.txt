That took two more fixes on top of the five from earlier:

6. **`taskflow/config.py` `deep_merge`:** the arguments in the recursive call were swapped, so nested overrides (such as a task's own retry settings) were overwritten by the defaults instead of replacing them. I changed it to `deep_merge(result[key], value)`. This fix is what made the retry tests pass: tasks were getting the default retry limit instead of their own, which is why attempts stayed at 1 even after the `should_retry` fix.
7. **`taskflow/scheduler.py` `_cascade_skip`:** when a task failed for good, only the tasks depending on it directly were marked skipped. The docstring says everything downstream should be skipped, so it now uses `self.dag.transitive_dependents(...)` instead of `dependents(...)`. This fixed the diamond and chain skip tests and the history skipped counts.
