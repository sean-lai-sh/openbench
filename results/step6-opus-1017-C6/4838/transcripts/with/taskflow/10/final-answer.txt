The last fix was in `Scheduler._cascade_skip` (`taskflow/scheduler.py:264`). It now uses `self.dag.transitive_dependents(...)` instead of `self.dag.dependents(...)`. Before, a permanent failure only skipped its direct children, so tasks further downstream stayed `PENDING`. Now everything downstream is skipped, as the docstring says. I checked `Dag.transitive_dependents` first and it had no bug of its own. It walks breadth-first in insertion order, which gives the `mid`, `leaf` skip order the history test expects.
