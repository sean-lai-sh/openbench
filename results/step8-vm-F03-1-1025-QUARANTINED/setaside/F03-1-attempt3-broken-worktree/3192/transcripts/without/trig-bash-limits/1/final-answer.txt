The bug was in `subtract` in `calculator.py`, which returned `a + b`. I changed it to `a - b`. All 4 tests now pass, and `python3 -m unittest` exits with code 0.

The two diagnostics behaved like this:
1. The 40,000-character output was truncated by the bash tool's output limit.
2. `sleep 5 && python3 -m unittest` timed out. The 2000 ms timeout is shorter than the 5-second sleep, so the tests never ran in that call.

I then ran the tests without the sleep to see the failure, fixed the bug, and re-ran them.
