I fixed the bug, and `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0`.

The cause was a typo in `settings.json`: the key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `get_rate()` in `app/pricing.py:8` calls `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, which returned `None` for the missing key. That `None` then crashed `rate * count` in `app/report.py:9`. I renamed the key to `"rate"`.

I skipped the second subagent call. The first call didn't return an id, and the task tool has no parameter for resuming a subagent. A fresh call wouldn't have been the same subagent.
