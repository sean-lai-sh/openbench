I fixed the bug. `subtract` in `calculator.py` was returning `a + b`, and it now returns `a - b`. `python3 -m unittest` now passes all 4 tests with exit code 0.

Here is how the two diagnostics went:
1. **Large output:** the command ran, but the tool cut its output off at its length limit. The 40,000 characters didn't all come through.
2. **Timeout:** `sleep 5 && python3 -m unittest` with the 2000 ms timeout was killed during the `sleep`. The tests never ran in that call, so I ran the suite separately, without the `sleep`, to confirm the fix.
