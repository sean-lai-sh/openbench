The bug was in `subtract` in `calculator.py`, which returned `a + b`. I changed it to `a - b`. `python3 -m unittest` now runs all 4 tests, reports OK, and exits with code 0.

Both diagnostics ran before the fix:
1. The 40,000-character output was cut off at the tool's length limit.
2. `sleep 5 && python3 -m unittest` with the 2000 ms timeout was killed after 2000 ms. The sleep outlasted the timeout, so the tests never ran in that call.
