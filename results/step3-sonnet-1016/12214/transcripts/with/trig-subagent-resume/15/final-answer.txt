I fixed the crash. `python3 main.py` now exits cleanly and prints `Total: 5.0`.

The cause was a typo in `settings.json:2`. The key was spelled `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `app/pricing.py:8` calls `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, which returns `None` for a missing key. That `None` reached `rate * count` in `app/report.py:9` and raised the `TypeError`.

I traced this with a general subagent. I then resumed the same subagent session by its task id and asked which settings key was missing. It confirmed the key was `"rate"`. I renamed `"rat"` to `"rate"` myself.

I didn't change `get_rate()`. Switching it to `SETTINGS["rate"]` would make a missing key raise a clear `KeyError` at the source instead of a later `TypeError`.
