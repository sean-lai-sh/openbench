`python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0` and exits with code 0.

The crash came from a typo in `settings.json:2`: the key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `app/pricing.py:8` calls `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, which returned `None` for the missing key. That `None` then hit `rate * count` in `app/report.py:9` and raised the `TypeError`.

I did the two subagent steps first. The first call traced `rate` to that line. I resumed the same subagent with its task id, and it confirmed the missing key is `"rate"`. I fixed it by renaming the key in `settings.json`.

I left `SETTINGS.get("rate")` in `app/pricing.py` as it was. Changing it to `SETTINGS["rate"]` would make a future missing key fail with a clear `KeyError` at the lookup.
