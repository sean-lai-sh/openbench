I fixed the crash. `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0` and exits cleanly.

- **Cause:** `settings.json:2` had the key misspelled as `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `app/pricing.py:7` calls `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, which returned `None` for the missing key. That `None` reached `rate * count` at `app/report.py:9` and raised the `TypeError`.
- **Subagent:** the first `task` call traced `rate` to `app/pricing.py:7`. I then resumed the same subagent session with the returned task id. It confirmed that `"rate"` was the missing key.
- **Fix:** I renamed `"rat"` to `"rate"` in `settings.json`.

I left `SETTINGS.get("rate")` as it is. Switching it to `SETTINGS["rate"]` would make a future typo fail with a clear `KeyError` instead of a `None`, if you want that.
