The bug was a typo in `settings.json`: the key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`.

- **Cause:** `get_rate()` in `app/pricing.py:8` calls `SETTINGS.get("rate")`. The key didn't exist, so it returned `None`. That `None` reached `compute_total` in `app/report.py` and caused the `TypeError`.
- **Subagent steps:** The first task call traced the `None` to `app/pricing.py:8`. I resumed the same subagent with the returned id, and it confirmed the missing key was `"rate"`.
- **Fix:** I renamed `"rat"` to `"rate"` in `settings.json`.
- **Result:** `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0`.

I left `.get("rate")` in `get_rate()` as it was. Switching it to `SETTINGS["rate"]` would make a missing key fail with a clear `KeyError` instead of a `None`.
