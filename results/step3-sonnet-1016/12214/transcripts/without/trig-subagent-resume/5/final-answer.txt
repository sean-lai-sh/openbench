The crash is fixed. `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0`.

The subagent found that `settings.json` had the key `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. Because `app/pricing.py:8` uses `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, the missing key returned `None` instead of raising an error. That `None` then hit `rate * count` in `app/report.py:9`. When I resumed the same subagent session, it confirmed that `"rate"` was the missing key.

I renamed the key to `"rate"` in `settings.json`. I left `pricing.py` alone. Changing `.get("rate")` to `SETTINGS["rate"]` would make a future typo like this fail with a clear `KeyError`, if you want that.
