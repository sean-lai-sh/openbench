`python3 main.py` now exits cleanly and prints `Total: 5.0`.

The cause was a typo in `settings.json`. Line 2 had the key `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `get_rate()` in `app/pricing.py:8` calls `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, which returns `None` when the key is missing. That `None` reached `rate * count` in `app/report.py:9` and raised the `TypeError`.

The subagent traced it to that line. When I resumed the same session and asked which key was missing, it answered `rate`. I fixed it by renaming the key in `settings.json`.

I left `SETTINGS.get("rate")` as it is. Switching it to `SETTINGS["rate"]` would make a missing key fail with a clear `KeyError` instead of a confusing `NoneType` error. I didn't make that change because you only asked for the crash to be fixed.
