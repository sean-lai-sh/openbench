Fixed. The subagent found that `settings.json` had the key `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. That made `SETTINGS.get("rate")` in `app/pricing.py:8` return `None`, which crashed at `rate * count` in `app/report.py`. The second call, which resumed the same subagent session, confirmed `"rate"` was the missing key.

I renamed the key to `"rate"` in `settings.json`. `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0`.
