`python3 main.py` now exits cleanly and prints `Total: 5.0`.

The subagent traced the crash to a typo in `settings.json:2`. The key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `app/pricing.py:8` calls `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, which returned `None` for the missing key. That `None` then hit `rate * count` at `app/report.py:9`. When I resumed the same subagent session and asked which settings key was missing, it said `"rate"`.

I fixed it by renaming the key to `"rate"` in `settings.json`. I didn't change `app/pricing.py`. Switching it to `SETTINGS["rate"]` would make a missing key raise a `KeyError` instead of returning `None`, but I left that out.
