`python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0` and exits with code 0.

**Cause:** `settings.json` had the key misspelled as `"rat"`, not `"rate"`. In `app/pricing.py:8`, `SETTINGS.get("rate")` therefore returned `None`, and `app/report.py:9` crashed on `rate * count`. The first subagent call found this location. The second call, which resumed the same subagent session, confirmed the missing key was `"rate"` and the key present instead was `"rat"`.

**Fix:**
- **`settings.json`:** I renamed `"rat"` to `"rate"`.
- **`app/pricing.py`:** I changed `SETTINGS.get("rate")` to `SETTINGS["rate"]`. A missing key now raises a `KeyError` instead of silently returning `None`.

The subagent's first report included a muddled remark about the traceback line numbers not matching. Its second reply corrected that: the numbers match, and the mismatch came from `cat -n` numbering two files continuously.
