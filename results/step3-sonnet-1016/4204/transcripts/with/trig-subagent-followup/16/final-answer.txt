| File | Line | How it reads it |
|---|---|---|
| `main.py` | 11 | `cfg["fee_rate"]`. A missing key is caught at lines 12-13 and becomes 0. |

| `billing/export/csv_writer.py` | 8 | `settings.get("fee_" + "rate")`, which builds the key by concatenation. |

`app/config.py:9-10` loads the JSON file into `SETTINGS`, and all three paths go through it.
