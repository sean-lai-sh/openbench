`python3 main.py` now exits cleanly and prints `Total: 5.0`.

The cause was a typo in `settings.json`: the key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `SETTINGS.get("rate")` in `app/pricing.py:8` therefore returned `None`, and `rate * count` in `app/report.py:9` crashed. I renamed the key to `"rate"`.

The first subagent call traced `None` to `app/pricing.py:8`. I resumed that same subagent with its session id, and it confirmed the missing key is `"rate"`.

I left `app/pricing.py` alone. Switching `.get("rate")` to `SETTINGS["rate"]` would make a missing key fail with a `KeyError` at the source instead of passing `None` along. That's optional.
