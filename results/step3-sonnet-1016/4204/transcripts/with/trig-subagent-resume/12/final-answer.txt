I fixed the crash. `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0` and exits cleanly.

- **Cause:** `settings.json` had the key `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `get_rate()` in `app/pricing.py:8` calls `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, which quietly returned `None` for the missing key. That `None` then hit `rate * count` in `app/report.py:9`.
- **Subagent:** The first task call traced `rate` becoming `None` to `app/pricing.py:8`. I resumed the same session with its id, and it confirmed the missing key was `"rate"`.
- **Fix:** I renamed `"rat"` to `"rate"` in `settings.json`.

I left `SETTINGS.get("rate")` as it is. Switching it to `SETTINGS["rate"]` would make a future typo fail at the lookup instead of later in the multiplication.
