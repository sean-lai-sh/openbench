- **Cause:** `settings.json` had the key `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `get_rate()` in `app/pricing.py:8` calls `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, which quietly returned `None`. That `None` then crashed `rate * count` in `app/report.py:9`.
- **Subagent calls:** The first call traced `rate` becoming `None` to `app/pricing.py:8`. I resumed the same subagent with its session id, and it said the missing key is `"rate"` (the file has `"rat": 0.5` instead).
- **Fix:** I renamed `"rat"` to `"rate"` in `settings.json`.

I didn't change `get_rate()`. Switching it to `SETTINGS["rate"]` would make a missing key raise a clear `KeyError` instead of returning `None`, if you want that.
