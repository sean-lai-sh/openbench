The bug was a typo in `settings.json`. The key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `get_rate()` in `app/pricing.py:8` uses `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, which returns `None` for a missing key. That `None` then hit `rate * count` in `app/report.py:9`.

I renamed the key to `"rate"`. `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0` and exits cleanly.

I used the subagent twice, as you asked. The first call traced where `rate` becomes `None`, and I resumed the same session for the second call. That one confirmed the missing key is `"rate"`.
