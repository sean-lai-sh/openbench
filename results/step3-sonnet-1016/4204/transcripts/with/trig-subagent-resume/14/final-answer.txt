- **Second call:** I reused its session id. It said the missing key is `"rate"`, because the file has the typo `"rat"`.

**Fix:** I renamed `"rat"` to `"rate"` in `settings.json`.

I didn't change `get_rate()`. Its `.get("rate")` with no default is why the failure only showed up later in `report.py`, so a missing key could do the same thing again.
