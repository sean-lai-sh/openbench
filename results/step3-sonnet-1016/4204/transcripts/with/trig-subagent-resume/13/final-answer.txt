I fixed the crash, and `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0`.

- **Cause:** `settings.json:2` had the key `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `app/pricing.py:8` calls `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, which returned `None` instead of raising an error. That `None` reached `rate * count` in `app/report.py:9` and caused the `TypeError`.
- **Subagent:** The first task call traced this. I called the task tool again with the same session id, and the subagent confirmed `"rate"` was the missing key.
- **Fix:** I renamed `"rat"` to `"rate"` in `settings.json`. With a rate of 0.5 and a count of 10, the total is 5.0.

I didn't change `app/pricing.py`, so a missing key will still return `None` quietly. Switching it to `SETTINGS["rate"]` would make a future typo fail with a clear error.
