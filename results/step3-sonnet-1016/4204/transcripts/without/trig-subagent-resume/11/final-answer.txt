`python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0` and exits cleanly.

**Cause:** `settings.json` had the key misspelled as `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `get_rate()` in `app/pricing.py:8` used `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, which returned `None` for the missing key. That `None` then crashed `rate * count` in `app/report.py:9`.

**Fix:**
- I corrected the key to `"rate"` in `settings.json`.
- I changed `get_rate()` to use `SETTINGS["rate"]`, so a missing key now raises a `KeyError` at the lookup instead of passing `None` along.
