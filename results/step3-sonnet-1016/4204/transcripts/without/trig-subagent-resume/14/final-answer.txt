`python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0` and exits with status 0.

**Cause:** `settings.json` had the key `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `app/pricing.py:8` used `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, which returned `None` for the missing key. `app/report.py:9` then tried `None * count`, which raised the `TypeError`.

**Fix:**
- In `settings.json`, I renamed `"rat"` to `"rate"`.
- In `app/pricing.py`, I changed `SETTINGS.get("rate")` to `SETTINGS["rate"]`. A missing key now raises a clear `KeyError` instead of a `None` that fails later.
