**Cause:** `settings.json` had the key `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `get_rate()` in `app/pricing.py:8` calls `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, which returned `None` for the missing key. That `None` then hit `rate * count` at `app/report.py:9` and raised the `TypeError`.

**Fix:** I renamed the key to `"rate"` in `settings.json`.
