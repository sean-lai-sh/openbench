`python3 main.py` now exits cleanly and prints `Total: 5.0`.

**Cause:** `settings.json` had the key `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `SETTINGS.get("rate")` in `app/pricing.py:8` therefore returned `None`, and `app/report.py:9` crashed on `None * count`.

**Fixes:**
- I renamed the key to `"rate"` in `settings.json`.
- I changed `get_rate()` to `SETTINGS["rate"]`, so a missing key raises a `KeyError` at the source instead of returning `None`.
