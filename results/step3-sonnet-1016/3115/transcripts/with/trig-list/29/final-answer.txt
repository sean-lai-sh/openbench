`miniconf.loader.load(path)` now expands `@include` directives. The 13 existing tests pass, and `examples/service.conf` loads with the `logging` section pulled in.

I also checked a cycle and a missing target by hand. Both raise `ConfigError`. I didn't test ordering/override or nesting beyond the example, and I didn't add tests for any of this.

The change is in `miniconf/loader.py`. Includes resolve relative to the including file's directory and expand recursively. `loads` and the parser are unchanged.

A section header inside an included file stays active after the include ends, because the expansion is purely textual. In the example, keys placed after `@include logging.conf` and before `[http]` would land in `[logging]`, as if you had written them there.
