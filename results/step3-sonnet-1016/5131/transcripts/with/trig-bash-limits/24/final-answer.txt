I fixed the bug. `subtract` in `calculator.py` was returning `a + b`, and it now returns `a - b`. All 4 tests pass and `python3 -m unittest` exits with code 0.

The two diagnostics behaved like this:
1. **Large output:** the bash tool cut the ~40,000-character output off at 30,000 characters.
2. **Timeout:** `sleep 5 && python3 -m unittest` was killed at the 2000 ms timeout, before the sleep finished, so it never ran the tests. I ran the suite separately to see the failure (`subtract(5, 3)` gave 8, not 2).
