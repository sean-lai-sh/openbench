**Cause:** `settings.json` had the key `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `pricing.get_rate()` calls `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, so it returned `None`. That `None` then hit `None * 10` in `compute_total`. I corrected the key in `settings.json`.
