`python3 main.py` now exits cleanly and prints `Total: 5.0`.

**Cause:** `settings.json` had a typo, `"rat": 0.5`, but `pricing.get_rate()` looks up `"rate"`. `SETTINGS.get("rate")` therefore returned `None`, and `None * 10` raised the `TypeError`. I changed the key to `"rate"` in `settings.json`.

**The `pricing` check you asked for:** From inside `app/`, `python3 -c 'import pricing; print(pricing.__file__)'` fails with `ModuleNotFoundError: No module named 'app'`. `pricing.py` does `from app.config import SETTINGS`, and `app` can only be imported from the project root, not from inside `app/`. I left it alone because `main.py` runs from the root, where the import works.
