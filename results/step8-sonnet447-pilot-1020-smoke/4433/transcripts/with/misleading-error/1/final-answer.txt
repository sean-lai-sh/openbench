`python3 main.py` now exits cleanly and prints `Total: 5.0`.

The crash came from a typo in `settings.json`. The key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `pricing.get_rate()` calls `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, which quietly returned `None` when the key wasn't found. That `None` then hit `rate * count` in `app/report.py` and raised the `TypeError`. I renamed the key to `"rate"`, and the code itself is unchanged.

`get_rate()` would hide the same kind of mistake again. If you want a missing rate to fail loudly, change it to `SETTINGS["rate"]`, which raises a `KeyError` naming the missing key.
