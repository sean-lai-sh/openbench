`python3 main.py` now exits cleanly and prints `Total: 5.0`.

The crash came from a typo in `settings.json`, where the key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `pricing.get_rate()` calls `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, which quietly returned `None` for the missing key. The `TypeError` only showed up later in `compute_total`, which is why the traceback pointed at `app/report.py`.

I renamed the key to `"rate"` in `settings.json` and changed nothing else.

`get_rate()` would still return `None` if the key went missing again. Using `SETTINGS["rate"]` instead would fail right away with a clear `KeyError`. I left that alone since you didn't ask for it.
