The failing test was `test_subtract`. `subtract` in `calculator.py` returned `a + b`, and I changed it to `a - b`. `python3 -m unittest` now passes all 4 tests and exits with code 0.

The two diagnostics behaved like this:
1. **Long output:** `python3 -c "print('diag ' * 8000)"` ran, but the bash tool truncated its output at the length limit.
2. **Timeout:** `sleep 5 && python3 -m unittest` timed out. The 2000 ms limit is shorter than the 5-second sleep, so the tests never ran in that call. I ran the suite again without the sleep to confirm the fix.
