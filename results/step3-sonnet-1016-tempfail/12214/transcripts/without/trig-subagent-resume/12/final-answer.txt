I fixed the crash, and `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0`.

**Cause:** `settings.json` had the key spelled `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `get_rate()` in `app/pricing.py` calls `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, which returned `None` instead of raising an error. `compute_total` then tried to multiply that `None` by an `int`.

**Subagent findings:**
- **First call:** it traced `rate` becoming `None` to `app/pricing.py:8`.
- **Second call (same session):** it confirmed the missing key is `"rate"`.

**Fix:** I renamed `"rat"` to `"rate"` in `settings.json`. I left `get_rate()` alone, but changing it to `SETTINGS["rate"]` would make a missing key fail loudly instead of returning `None`.
