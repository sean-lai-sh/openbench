The bug was a typo in `settings.json`: line 2 had the key `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. I renamed the key, and `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0`.

The first subagent call traced it. `SETTINGS.get("rate")` in `app/pricing.py:8` returned `None` because the key didn't exist. That `None` reached `rate * count` in `app/report.py:9`. I resumed the same subagent session, and it confirmed `rate` was the missing key.

I left `.get("rate")` as it was. Switching it to `SETTINGS["rate"]` would make a future typo fail with a `KeyError` at the source instead of a `TypeError` later.
