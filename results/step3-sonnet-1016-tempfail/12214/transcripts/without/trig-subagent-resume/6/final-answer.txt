The bug is fixed. `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0`.

- **Cause:** `settings.json` had the key `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `get_rate()` in `app/pricing.py:27` calls `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, which returns `None` for a missing key. That `None` reached `rate * count` at `app/report.py:9` and raised the TypeError.
- **Subagent:** the first task call traced the `None` to that line. I resumed the same session (`ses_eb7fe75d7ffe3UlPkJRfRYHDYh`) and it confirmed the missing key is `"rate"`.
- **Fix:** I renamed `"rat"` to `"rate"` in `settings.json`.

I left `get_rate()` as it is. Changing it to `SETTINGS["rate"]` would make a future typo fail with a clear `KeyError` instead of a `None`.
