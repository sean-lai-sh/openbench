The bug was a typo in `settings.json`. Line 2 had the key `"rat"`, but `app/pricing.py` reads `"rate"`, so `rate` came back as `None`. The subagent found this. `app/report.py:8` gets `rate` from `pricing.get_rate()`, which returns `SETTINGS.get("rate")`.

I renamed the key to `"rate"` in `settings.json`. `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0`.
