The crash came from a typo in `settings.json`. The key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`, so `SETTINGS.get("rate")` returned `None`, and `None * 10` raised the `TypeError`.

I changed the key to `"rate"`. `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0` and exits cleanly.

I left the code alone. `get_rate()` in `app/pricing.py` still returns `None` silently when the key is missing. If you want a future typo like this to fail with a clear error, say so and I'll change it.
