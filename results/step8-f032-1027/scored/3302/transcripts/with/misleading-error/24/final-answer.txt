`python3 main.py` now exits cleanly and prints `Total: 5.0`.

The cause was a typo in `settings.json`: the key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `pricing.get_rate()` calls `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, which returned `None` because the key didn't exist. `compute_total` then tried to multiply `None` by an int. I renamed the key to `"rate"` and changed nothing else.
