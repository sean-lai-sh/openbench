`python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0` and exits cleanly.

The cause was a typo in `settings.json`: the key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `pricing.get_rate()` looks up `"rate"`, so it got `None`, and `compute_total` then failed on `None * 10`. I renamed the key to `"rate"` and changed no code.
