`python3 main.py` now exits cleanly and prints `Total: 5.0`.

`settings.json` had a typo: the key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `pricing.get_rate()` looks up `"rate"`, so `SETTINGS.get("rate")` returned `None`. That `None` then broke `rate * count` in `compute_total`. I renamed the key to `"rate"`, and I didn't change any code.
