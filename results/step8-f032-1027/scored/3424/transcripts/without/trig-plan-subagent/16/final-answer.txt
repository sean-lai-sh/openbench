The crash is fixed. `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0`.

The subagent traced it to a typo in the settings file. `settings.json:2` defined the key as `"rat"`, but `app/pricing.py:8` looks up `SETTINGS.get("rate")`. The lookup returned `None`, and `compute_total` then tried `None * count`.

I renamed the key to `"rate"` in `settings.json`. I didn't change any code.
