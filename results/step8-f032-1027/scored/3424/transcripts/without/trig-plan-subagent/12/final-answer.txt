The bug was a typo in `settings.json:2`: the key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `app/pricing.py:8` reads `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, which returned `None`, so `compute_total` ended up doing `None * 10`. The subagent found this and I checked it myself.

I renamed the key to `"rate"`. `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0`.
