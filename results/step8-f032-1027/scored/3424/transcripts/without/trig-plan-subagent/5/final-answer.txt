The crash came from a typo in `settings.json`. Line 2 had the key `"rat"`, but `app/pricing.py:8` looks up `"rate"`, so `SETTINGS.get("rate")` returned `None`. The subagent found this, and I confirmed it by reading the files.

I renamed the key to `"rate"` in `settings.json:2`. `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0` and exits cleanly.
