The crash came from a typo in `settings.json`. Line 2 had the key `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`.

The subagent traced it. `app/report.py:8` calls `pricing.get_rate()`, which does `SETTINGS.get("rate")` at `app/pricing.py:8`. With the misspelled key, that returned `None`, and `None * count` raised the `TypeError`.

I changed `"rat"` to `"rate"` in `settings.json:2`. `python3 main.py` now exits cleanly and prints `Total: 5.0`.
