The subagent found the cause, and `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0` and exits with code 0.

- **Cause:** `app/pricing.py:8` reads `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, but `settings.json:2` defined the key as `"rat"` (a typo). The lookup returned `None`, which crashed `rate * count` in `app/report.py:9`.
- **Fix:** I renamed the key to `"rate"` in `settings.json`. Its value is 0.5, and `COUNT` is 10, so the total is 5.0.
