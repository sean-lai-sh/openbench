The subagent found that `rate` should come from the `"rate"` key in `settings.json`. `app/pricing.py:8` reads it with `SETTINGS.get("rate")`. The file defined it as `"rat"` at `settings.json:2`, so the lookup returned `None` and `rate * count` crashed.

I renamed the key to `"rate"`. `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0`.
