The crash is fixed. `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0` and exits with code 0.

The subagent traced it to a misspelled key. `settings.json:2` had `"rat": 0.5`, but `app/pricing.py:8` looks up `SETTINGS.get("rate")`. The lookup returned `None`, which caused the `TypeError` in `app/report.py:9`.

I renamed the key to `"rate"` in `settings.json`.
