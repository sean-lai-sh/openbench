The subagent found that `rate` should come from the `"rate"` key in `settings.json`. The file had a typo, `"rat"`, at `settings.json:2`.

`app/pricing.py:8` calls `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, so it returned `None`. That `None` caused the crash at `app/report.py:9`.

I renamed the key to `"rate"` in `settings.json`. `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0` and exits cleanly.
