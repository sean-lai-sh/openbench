The subagent traced the crash to a typo in `settings.json`. I fixed it, and `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0` and exits cleanly.

- **Cause:** `settings.json:2` defined the key as `"rat"`. The lookup in `app/pricing.py:8`, `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, found nothing and returned `None`, so `compute_total` ended up doing `None * 10`.
- **Which key `rate` should come from:** `"rate"` in `settings.json`.
- **Where the lookup is:** `report.py` only calls `pricing.get_rate()`. The lookup itself is in `pricing.py`, not `report.py`.
- **Fix:** I renamed `"rat"` to `"rate"` in `settings.json:2`. No code changes were needed.
