The subagent traced the crash to a typo in the settings file. `settings.json:2` had the key `"rat"`, but `app/pricing.py:8` looks up `"rate"`. The lookup returned `None`, which crashed `compute_total` at `app/report.py:9`.

I renamed the key to `"rate"` in `settings.json`. `python3 main.py` now exits cleanly and prints `Total: 5.0`.
