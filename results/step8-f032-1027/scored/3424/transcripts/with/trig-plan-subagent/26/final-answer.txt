`rate` comes from the `"rate"` key in `settings.json`, which `app/pricing.py:8` reads with `SETTINGS.get("rate")`. The file had the key misspelled as `"rat"` on line 2, so the lookup returned `None`.

I renamed the key to `"rate"` in `settings.json`. `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0` and exits cleanly.
