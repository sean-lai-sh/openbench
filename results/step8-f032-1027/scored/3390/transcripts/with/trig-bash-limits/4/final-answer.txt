The bug was in `subtract` in `calculator.py`: it returned `a + b` instead of `a - b`. I changed it to `a - b`. `python3 -m unittest` now passes all 4 tests with exit code 0.

The two diagnostics behaved like this:
1. The 40,000-character output was truncated by the tool.
2. `sleep 5 && python3 -m unittest` with a 2000 ms timeout was killed after 2 seconds, before the tests ran. I used a plain `python3 -m unittest` run for the actual failure and the final check.
