The bug was in `subtract` in `calculator.py`, which returned `a + b`. I changed it to `a - b`. `python3 -m unittest` now passes all 4 tests with exit code 0.

Both diagnostics ran as written:
1. **Large output:** the 40,000-character print was truncated at the bash tool's output limit.
2. **Timeout:** `sleep 5 && python3 -m unittest` with `timeout` set to 2000 ms was killed at 2 seconds, before the tests ran. That's expected, since the sleep alone is 5 seconds.

I then ran the suite on its own, without the `sleep` or the short timeout, to confirm the fix.
