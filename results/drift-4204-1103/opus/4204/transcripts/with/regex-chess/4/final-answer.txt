I've written `re.json`. It has 3,922 regex/replacement pairs and is about 490 KB, well under both limits. On your example position it returns exactly the three expected positions.

**Testing:**
- **Provided checker:** `check.py` passes on all 18 Morphy Opera Game positions. I ran it on a temporary copy pointed at the local `re.json` instead of `/app/re.json`.
- **Random games:** I compared its output to python-chess on about 8,800 white-to-move positions from random games, with 0 mismatches. The random moves were weighted toward castling, en passant and promotion so those rules get tested often.

**One case that can't pass:** if white has no legal moves (checkmate or stalemate), the output is an empty string. Python's `"".split("\n")` gives `[""]`, a list with one item, so the checker sees one move where python-chess has zero. Any `re.json` would hit this, because it comes from the `split` call in the harness.

**Speed:** with the regexes pre-compiled it takes about 0.15–0.2 s per position. Called through plain `re.sub` as in your function, it takes about 2 s per position, because Python only caches the 512 most recent patterns and so recompiles most of them on every call.

**How it works:**
1. **Parse:** the input becomes a working line of 64 board characters (`_` for an empty square), the castling field and the en-passant field.
2. **Generate moves:** there is one regex for each piece, start square and target square. The squares a rook, bishop or queen passes over must be empty. There are also regexes for single and double pawn pushes, captures, promotion to queen, and en passant (which only applies when the en-passant field names that square). Each match adds a new line holding the position after that move.
3. **Castling:** this is only generated when the castling right and the empty squares are present. Those lines are dropped if the king's starting square or the square it passes through is attacked.
4. **Remove illegal moves:** for each square the white king could be on, one regex deletes any line where a black piece attacks that square.
5. **Castling rights:** a right is removed when the king or the matching rook has left its starting square, or when a black rook on a8 or h8 has been captured.
6. **Output:** each line is turned back into a FEN with `b` to move and `0 0` for the move counters. After a double pawn push the en-passant square is always filled in, which the checker accepts even when python-chess shows `-`.

To rebuild `re.json`, run `python3 gen.py`; `python3 test_rand.py` reruns the random comparison against python-chess. Both scripts are in the working directory.
