**Cause:** `settings.json` had the key `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. So `pricing.get_rate()`, which calls `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, quietly returned `None`, and `compute_total` then failed on `None * 10`.

**Fix:** I renamed the key to `"rate"` in `settings.json`. With 10 items at 0.5 each, the total is 5.0.

A missing key still won't raise an error at the point where it's read. If you'd rather have it fail loudly, `get_rate()` could use `SETTINGS["rate"]` instead of `.get("rate")`. I haven't made that change. body
