The crash came from a typo in the settings file, and it's fixed: `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0` and exits with code 0.

**What the subagent found** (I asked it twice, and the second call reused the same session):
- **Where `rate` becomes None:** line 2 of `settings.json` spelled the key `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. Line 8 of `app/pricing.py` reads it with `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, which returns `None` when the key isn't there instead of raising an error. That `None` reached `rate * count` at line 9 of `app/report.py` and caused the TypeError.
- **Which settings key is missing:** `"rate"`.

**Fix:** I renamed `"rat"` to `"rate"` on line 2 of `settings.json`.

**Optional change:** if `app/pricing.py:8` used `SETTINGS["rate"]` instead of `.get("rate")`, a missing key would stop the program right away with a clear KeyError rather than this confusing message. I didn't make that change.
