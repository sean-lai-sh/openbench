The crash came from a typo in `settings.json`, and I've fixed it. `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0` and exits with code 0.

- **First subagent call:** it found that `rate` becomes `None` at `app/pricing.py:8`, in `return SETTINGS.get("rate")`. When a key is missing, `.get` returns `None` instead of raising an error, so `None * 10` then failed at `app/report.py:9`.
- **Second call (same subagent, via its session id):** it confirmed the missing key is `"rate"`. Line 2 of `settings.json` had `"rat": 0.5` instead.
- **Fix:** I renamed the key to `"rate"` in `settings.json:2`.

Optionally, `get_rate()` could use `SETTINGS["rate"]` so a missing key fails right away with a clear error. I didn't make that change.
