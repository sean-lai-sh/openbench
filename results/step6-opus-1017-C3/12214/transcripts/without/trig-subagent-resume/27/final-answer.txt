**Missing key:** When I resumed the same subagent (session `ses_eb2247720ffetGZwwzawY54vDw`), it confirmed the missing key is `"rate"`.

**Fix:** I renamed `"rat"` to `"rate"` in `settings.json`.

You could also change `pricing.py:8` to `SETTINGS["rate"]`, so a missing key fails straight away with a clear `KeyError`. I didn't make that change.
