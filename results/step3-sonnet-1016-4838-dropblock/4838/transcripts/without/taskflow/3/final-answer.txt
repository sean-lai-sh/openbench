All 25 tests now pass with `python3 -m unittest`.

The last three failures came from one bug in `taskflow/resources.py`. `ResourcePool.release` ran `self.used -= 1` instead of `self.used -= cost`. A task with a cost above 1 gave back only one unit when it finished. The pool then looked permanently full, tasks waiting on it never ran, and `report.ok()` came back False.
