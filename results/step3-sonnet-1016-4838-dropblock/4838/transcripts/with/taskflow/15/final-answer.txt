The suite passes: 25 tests ran and all of them passed, so there's nothing left to do. I didn't modify any tests.

The seven fixes, all under `taskflow/`:
1. **`queue.py`**: `_Entry.sort_key` is now `(-priority, seq)`, so higher priority runs first and ties go in FIFO order.
2. **`retry.py`**: `should_retry` now uses `attempts_made < max_attempts`. The old off-by-one allowed one attempt too few.
3. **`events.py`**: `_prefix_matches` had its arguments reversed. A prefix subscription now matches the topics under it.
4. **`statemachine.py`**: `batch_order_ok` now requires non-increasing priority within a batch. The old check made the scheduler's admission guard fail runs wrongly.
5. **`resources.py`**: `release` now subtracts `cost` instead of 1.
6. **`config.py`**: `deep_merge` had the override and base swapped in its recursive call, so overrides now win in nested dicts.
7. **`scheduler.py`**: `_cascade_skip` now uses `transitive_dependents`, so the whole downstream subtree is skipped, not just direct children.
