The subagent traced the crash to a typo in `settings.json`, and I fixed it. `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0`.

- **Cause:** line 2 of `settings.json` defined the key as `"rat"`. The lookup `SETTINGS.get("rate")` at `app/pricing.py:8` therefore returned `None`, and `rate * count` in `app/report.py:9` raised the `TypeError`.
- **Fix:** I renamed the key to `"rate"` in `settings.json:2`. With `COUNT` at 10, that gives 0.5 × 10 = 5.0.
