Typo in the settings key: `rat` instead of `rate`.


`python3 main.py` now exits cleanly and prints `Total: 5.0`.

The cause was a typo in `settings.json`: the key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `get_rate()` in `app/pricing.py` uses `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, which returns `None` for a missing key. That `None` then hit `rate * count` in `compute_total` and raised the `TypeError`. I renamed the key to `"rate"` and didn't change any code.
