Key typo: `"rat"` should be `"rate"`.


`python3 main.py` now exits cleanly and prints `Total: 5.0`.

The cause was a typo in `settings.json`: the key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `pricing.get_rate()` looks up `"rate"` with `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, which returned `None` when the key was missing. That `None` was then multiplied by the count, which raised the `TypeError`. I renamed the key to `"rate"` and changed nothing else.
