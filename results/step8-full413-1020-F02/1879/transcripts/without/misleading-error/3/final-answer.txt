The settings key is misspelled ("rat" instead of "rate"), so `get("rate")` returns None.


The crash came from a typo in `settings.json`. The key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`, so `SETTINGS.get("rate")` in `app/pricing.py` returned `None`. Then `compute_total` tried to multiply `None` by an int.

I renamed the key to `"rate"`. `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0` and exits cleanly.
