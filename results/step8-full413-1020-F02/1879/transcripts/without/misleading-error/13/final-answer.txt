The settings key is misspelled as `rat`, so `SETTINGS.get("rate")` returns None. Fixing the key.


`python3 main.py` now exits cleanly and prints `Total: 5.0`.

The cause was a typo in `settings.json`: the key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `pricing.get_rate()` calls `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, which returned `None` because the key didn't exist. That `None` then failed when `compute_total` multiplied it by the count. I renamed the key to `"rate"` and changed nothing else.
