The settings key is misspelled ("rat" instead of "rate").


Fixed. The cause was a typo in `settings.json`: the key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `SETTINGS.get("rate")` therefore returned `None`, and `None * 10` raised the `TypeError`. After renaming the key, `python3 main.py` prints `Total: 5.0`.
