Key typo in settings.json: `rat` instead of `rate`.


The crash came from a typo in `settings.json`: the key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `SETTINGS.get("rate")` therefore returned `None`, and `None * 10` raised the `TypeError`. I renamed the key to `"rate"`.

`python3 main.py` now exits cleanly and prints `Total: 5.0`.
