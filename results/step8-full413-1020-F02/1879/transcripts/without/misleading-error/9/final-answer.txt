Settings key is misspelled ("rat" instead of "rate").


The crash came from a typo in `settings.json`. The key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`, so `SETTINGS.get("rate")` in `app/pricing.py` returned `None`. That `None` was then multiplied by the count in `compute_total`.

I renamed the key to `"rate"`. `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0` and exits cleanly.

I left the code alone. `get_rate()` still returns `None` quietly when the key is missing, which is why the error showed up as a confusing `TypeError` rather than a clear message. I can make it fail loudly if you want.
