The settings key is misspelled ("rat" instead of "rate").


The crash came from a typo in `settings.json`: the key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `SETTINGS.get("rate")` therefore returned `None`, and `None * 10` raised the `TypeError`. I corrected the key, and `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0`.
