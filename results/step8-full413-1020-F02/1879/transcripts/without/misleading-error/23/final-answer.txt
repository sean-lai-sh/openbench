Key typo in settings.json: `"rat"` should be `"rate"`.


`python3 main.py` now exits successfully and prints `Total: 5.0`.

The cause was a typo in `settings.json`: the key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `SETTINGS.get("rate")` in `app/pricing.py` therefore returned `None`, and `compute_total` then tried to multiply `None` by an int. I renamed the key to `"rate"` and left the code unchanged.
