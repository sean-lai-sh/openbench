Root cause: the settings key is misspelled (`rat` instead of `rate`), so `.get("rate")` returns None.


The crash came from a typo in `settings.json`. The key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`, so `SETTINGS.get("rate")` in `app/pricing.py` returned `None`. That `None` then hit `rate * count` in `compute_total`.

I renamed the key to `"rate"`. `python3 main.py` now exits cleanly and prints `Total: 5.0`.
