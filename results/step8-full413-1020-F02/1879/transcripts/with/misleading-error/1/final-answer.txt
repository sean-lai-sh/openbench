Typo in the settings key: `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`.


The crash came from a typo in `settings.json`. The key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`, so `SETTINGS.get("rate")` returned `None`. That `None` was then multiplied by the count. I renamed the key to `"rate"`, and `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0` and exits cleanly.
