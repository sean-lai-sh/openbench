Typo in the settings key: `rat` instead of `rate`.


The crash came from a typo in `settings.json`. The key was `"rat"`, not `"rate"`. `SETTINGS.get("rate")` therefore returned `None`, and `None * 10` raised the `TypeError`.

I renamed the key to `"rate"`. `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0` and exits cleanly.
