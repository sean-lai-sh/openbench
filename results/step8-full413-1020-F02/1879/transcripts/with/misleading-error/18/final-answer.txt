Typo in settings key: `rat` instead of `rate`.


Fixed. The cause was a typo in `settings.json`: the key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `SETTINGS.get("rate")` therefore returned `None`, which crashed `rate * count`. I renamed the key, and `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0`.
