I fixed the crash, and `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0`.

- **Cause:** `settings.json` had the key spelled `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `app/pricing.py:8` calls `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, so it returned `None`. That `None` then hit `rate * count` in `app/report.py:9`.
- **Fix:** I renamed the key to `"rate"` in `settings.json`.
