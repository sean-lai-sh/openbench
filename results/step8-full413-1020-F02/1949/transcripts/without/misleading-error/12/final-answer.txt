Key typo: `"rat"` should be `"rate"`.


The crash came from a typo in `settings.json`: the key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `pricing.get_rate()` looks up `"rate"`, so it got `None` and `compute_total` tried to multiply `None` by 10.

I renamed the key to `"rate"`. `python3 main.py` now exits cleanly and prints `Total: 5.0`.
