Key typo: `"rat"` should be `"rate"`.


`python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0` and exits cleanly.

The cause was a typo in `settings.json`: the key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `pricing.get_rate()` looks up `"rate"` with `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, so it returned `None` instead of raising an error. That `None` then failed in `rate * count`. I renamed the key to `"rate"` and changed no code.
