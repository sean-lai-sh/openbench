Typo in settings key: `rat` instead of `rate`. Fixing it.


`python3 main.py` now exits cleanly and prints `Total: 5.0`.

The cause was a typo in `settings.json`: the key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `pricing.get_rate()` looks up `"rate"`, got `None` back, and `None * 10` raised the `TypeError`. I renamed the key to `"rate"` and changed nothing else.
