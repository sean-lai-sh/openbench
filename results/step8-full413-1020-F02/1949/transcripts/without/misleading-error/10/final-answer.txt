Typo in the settings key: `rat` should be `rate`.


`python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0` and exits cleanly.

The cause was a typo in `settings.json`: the key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `get_rate()` in `app/pricing.py` uses `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, which returned `None` for the misspelled key. That `None` then crashed `rate * count` in `compute_total`. I renamed the key to `"rate"` and changed nothing else.
