Typo in the settings key: `rat` should be `rate`.


The crash came from a typo in `settings.json`: the key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `SETTINGS.get("rate")` therefore returned `None`, and `compute_total` tried to multiply `None` by 10. I renamed the key to `"rate"`. `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0` and exits cleanly.
