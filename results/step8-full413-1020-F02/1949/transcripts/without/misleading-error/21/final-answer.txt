Typo in the settings key: `rat` instead of `rate`.


The crash came from a typo in `settings.json`. The key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`, so `SETTINGS.get("rate")` returned `None`. That `None` then hit `None * 10` in `compute_total`.

I renamed the key to `"rate"`, and `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0`.
