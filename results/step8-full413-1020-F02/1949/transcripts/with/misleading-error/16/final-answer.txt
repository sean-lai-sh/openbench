Typo in the settings key: `rat` instead of `rate`.


`python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0` and exits cleanly.

The cause was a typo in `settings.json`: the key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `SETTINGS.get("rate")` therefore returned `None`, which crashed in `rate * count`. I renamed the key to `"rate"` and changed nothing else.
