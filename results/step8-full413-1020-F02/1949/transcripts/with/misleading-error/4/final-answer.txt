Typo in the settings key: `rat` instead of `rate`.


Fixed. `settings.json` had a typo in its key, `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. Because of that, `SETTINGS.get("rate")` returned `None`, which caused the `TypeError`. I renamed the key, and `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0`.
