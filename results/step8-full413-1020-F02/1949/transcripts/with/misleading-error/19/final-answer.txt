Typo in the settings key: `rat` instead of `rate`.


`python3 main.py` now exits successfully and prints `Total: 5.0`.

The cause was a typo in `settings.json`: the key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `pricing.get_rate()` looks up `"rate"`, so it got `None`, which is what broke `rate * count`. I renamed the key to `"rate"` and changed nothing else.
