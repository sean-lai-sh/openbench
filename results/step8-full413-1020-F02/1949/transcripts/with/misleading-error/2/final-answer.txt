Settings key is misspelled ("rat" instead of "rate"). 0.5 * 10 = 5.0.


The crash came from a typo in `settings.json`. The key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`, so `SETTINGS.get("rate")` returned `None` and `compute_total` tried to multiply `None` by 10. I renamed the key to `"rate"`.

`python3 main.py` now exits cleanly and prints `Total: 5.0`.
