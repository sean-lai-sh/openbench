Typo in the settings key: `rat` should be `rate`.


The crash came from a typo in `settings.json`. The key was `"rat"`, but `pricing.get_rate()` looks up `"rate"`. `SETTINGS.get("rate")` therefore returned `None`, and `None * count` raised the `TypeError`.

I renamed the key to `"rate"`. `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0` and exits cleanly.

I didn't change any code. If you'd rather a missing rate fail with a clear error than return `None`, `get_rate()` could raise instead.
