Key typo: `"rat"` should be `"rate"`. Fixing it gives 0.5 × 10 = 5.0.


The crash came from a typo in `settings.json`: the key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `get_rate()` looks up `"rate"`, found nothing, and returned `None`. That `None` then hit `None * 10` in `compute_total`.

I renamed the key to `"rate"`. `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0` and exits cleanly.
