Typo in the settings key: `rat` should be `rate`.


`python3 main.py` now exits cleanly and prints `Total: 5.0`.

The cause was a typo in `settings.json`: the key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `pricing.get_rate()` looks up `"rate"` with `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, which returned `None`. That `None` is what `compute_total` then tried to multiply. I renamed the key to `"rate"` and left the code alone.
