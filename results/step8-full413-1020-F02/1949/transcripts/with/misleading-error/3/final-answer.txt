Key typo: "rat" instead of "rate".


`settings.json` had a typo: the key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `SETTINGS.get("rate")` therefore returned `None`, which caused the `TypeError`. I renamed the key to `"rate"`, and `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0`.
