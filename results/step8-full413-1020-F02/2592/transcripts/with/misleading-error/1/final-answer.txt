The settings key is misspelled ("rat" instead of "rate").


The crash came from a typo in `settings.json`: the key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `pricing.get_rate()` looks up `"rate"`, found nothing, and returned `None`. That `None` was then multiplied by the count in `compute_total`.

I renamed the key to `"rate"`. `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0` and exits cleanly.
