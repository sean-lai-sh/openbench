The bug was a typo in `settings.json`. Line 2 had the key `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. I found this by having a subagent trace where `rate` comes from.

Here is how the crash happened:
- `app/pricing.py:8` calls `SETTINGS.get("rate")`. The key didn't exist, so it returned `None` instead of raising an error.
- `app/report.py:9` then ran `rate * count` with `rate` set to `None`, which raised the `TypeError`.

I changed `"rat"` to `"rate"` in `settings.json`. `python3 main.py` now exits cleanly and prints `Total: 5.0`.
