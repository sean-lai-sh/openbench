The crash came from a typo in `settings.json:2`. The key was `"rat"` instead of `"rate"`. `app/pricing.py:8` calls `SETTINGS.get("rate")`, which returned `None` instead of raising an error. `app/report.py:9` then multiplied `None` by `COUNT`, which caused the `TypeError`.

I renamed the key to `"rate"`. `python3 main.py` now prints `Total: 5.0` and exits cleanly.
